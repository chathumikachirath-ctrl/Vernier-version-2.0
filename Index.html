How to read it

§ MAIN SCALE

Fixed scale, in mm

Engraved on the beam. Take the Main Scale Reading (MSR) as the mark just before — to the left of — the vernier's zero line.

§ VERNIER SCALE

Sliding scale, 10 divisions

10 vernier divisions span exactly 9 mm, so each division is 0.9 mm. Find the one line that sits exactly level with a main‑scale mark — that division number is the Vernier Coincidence (VC).

§ LEAST COUNT

Smallest reading you can trust

LC = 1 MSD − 1 VSD = 1 mm − 0.9 mm = 0.1 mm

Reading = MSR + (VC × LC)

§ ZERO ERROR

Check before you trust the scale

Close the jaws fully. If the vernier zero doesn't land exactly on the main‑scale zero, the instrument has a zero error.

Corrected reading = Observed reading − Zero error
